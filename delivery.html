Delivery Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy applies to the physical delivery of prizes won through Pixel Pick competitions.

5.1 Delivery area and cost

Prize delivery is free of charge within the Republic of South Africa. Pixel Pick does not charge a winner a delivery fee for a prize delivered to a South African address.

5.2 Confirming delivery details

A verified winner may be asked to provide or confirm a South African delivery address and contact details needed to arrange delivery. Winners are responsible for supplying accurate and complete information and for notifying Pixel Pick if those details change before dispatch. Winners may also be required to provide additional identity verification documents before a prize is released.

5.3 Delivery arrangements and timing

Pixel Pick will contact the verified winner to coordinate delivery. The expected dispatch or delivery arrangements will be communicated once the prize and delivery details have been confirmed. Delivery timing may depend on the prize, courier availability, the delivery destination and circumstances outside Pixel Pick's reasonable control.

5.4 Receiving the prize

The winner or an authorised recipient should be available to receive the delivery. If delivery cannot be completed because the address is incorrect, the recipient is unavailable or delivery details have not been provided, Pixel Pick will contact the winner to arrange the next reasonable step.

5.5 Prize condition and discrepancies

If a prize arrives damaged or is materially different from the prize description, contact info@pixelpickcustomer.co.za as soon as reasonably possible and provide the competition details and a description of the issue. This policy does not limit any rights available under applicable law.

5.6 Prize restrictions and tax

A prize is awarded to the verified winner identified through the competition results. Unless the competition page states otherwise or applicable law requires otherwise, a prize may not be exchanged for cash or transferred to another person. Any tax liability arising from the receipt of a prize is the responsibility of the winner, unless Pixel Pick is required by law to withhold tax.
