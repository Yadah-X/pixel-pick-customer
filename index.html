YOUR GUIDE TO PIXEL PICK

How to Play

Ready to compete? Here's everything you need to know about entering a Pixel Pick competition and how our winners are determined.

1. Choose a Competition

Browse our available competitions and select the prize you would like to compete for. Each competition displays its prize details and entry fee.

2. Register or Sign In

Create a Pixel Pick account or sign in to your existing account before entering a competition.

Participants must be South African residents aged 18 years or older.

3. Complete the Skill Challenge

Each competition entry gives you one attempt at a visual skill challenge consisting of 10 tasks.

  • Study each visual task carefully.
  • Select the answer you believe is correct.
  • Answer each task only once.
  • Complete all 10 tasks within the allocated time.

Once your attempt has been submitted, you cannot replay that attempt to improve your result.

4. Complete Your Entry

After completing the skill challenge, follow the instructions to complete your entry.

Only successfully paid entries qualify for the competition. Unpaid or failed payments do not qualify.

5. How the Winner Is Determined

Winners are determined according to the following criteria, in this order:

  1. Highest skill score: The participant with the highest overall score wins.
  2. Highest accuracy: If participants have equal scores, the participant with the highest accuracy takes priority.
  3. First to complete: If both skill score and accuracy are equal, the first participant to complete the challenge takes priority.

Pixel Pick will review the results and approve the winner before announcing them.

6. Results and Winner Announcement

Your challenge results will be securely recorded. Completing a challenge does not automatically mean that you have won.

The winner will be confirmed after the competition results have been reviewed.

7. Important Information

  • Each entry is a separate competition attempt.
  • Only eligible participants may enter.
  • Only qualifying entries are considered when determining a winner.
  • All participants must follow the Pixel Pick Terms and Conditions.

Good luck, and remember: Skill. Precision. Prizes.
