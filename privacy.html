Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixel Pick collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you visit the website, create an account, enter a competition, use the referral programme or contact support. Pixel Pick aims to handle personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

2.1 Personal information we may collect

  • Account information, such as your first name, last name and email address.
  • Optional contact information that you choose to provide, such as a phone number.
  • Competition information, including entries, challenge responses, scores, accuracy, completion times and results.
  • Referral information, including referral codes, referral activity and reward records.
  • Payment and transaction information made available to Pixel Pick, such as payment status, amount and transaction references.
  • Technical and security information, such as device or browser information, access logs and information needed to maintain account sessions and protect the website.

The information collected will depend on the features you use and the information you choose to provide.

2.2 How we use personal information

  • To create and manage accounts and provide access to the website.
  • To process entries and administer skill competitions.
  • To record challenge answers, calculate results and verify competition rankings.
  • To process and reconcile payments, free entries and referral rewards.
  • To communicate about accounts, entries, competitions, prizes, support requests and important service updates.
  • To detect fraud, prevent abuse, maintain security and enforce the website's terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

2.3 When information may be shared

Pixel Pick may share information where reasonably necessary with service providers that support website hosting, database services, payment processing, security, communications or technical operations. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect the rights or safety of participants, or to investigate suspected fraud or misuse. Service providers should receive only the information reasonably needed for their role.

2.4 Payment information

Payments may be handled by an external payment service provider. The provider's own privacy and security terms apply to information collected directly by that provider. Pixel Pick may receive transaction details and payment status needed to confirm entries, reconcile payments and assist with payment queries.

2.5 Storage and security

Pixel Pick uses reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Personal information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account administration, competition records, dispute resolution, security and legal requirements.

Indicative retention periods include:

  • Active account data for the life of the account plus 2 years after closure;
  • Competition records for 5 years after the competition ends (or longer if required for legal or dispute purposes); and
  • Payment and transaction records for the period required by applicable financial and tax laws (typically 5 years).

2.6 Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal information held about you, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, object to certain processing, or request deletion where the information is no longer required or where deletion is otherwise permitted. You may also raise a concern about how your personal information is handled. Requests can be sent to info@pixelpickcustomer.co.za. Pixel Pick may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

2.7 Cookies and local storage

The website may use essential browser storage or similar technologies to support account sessions, remember necessary website activity, maintain security and support features such as referral links. You can manage browser storage through your browser settings, although disabling essential storage may affect website functionality.

2.8 Children

Pixel Pick competitions are intended for people aged 18 and older. The website is not intended to collect personal information from children for participation in competitions.

2.9 Cross-border transfers

Some of the service providers we use to host the website, store data, process payments or send communications may be located outside the Republic of South Africa. Where personal information is transferred outside South Africa, we take reasonable steps to ensure that the recipient provides a level of protection that is substantially similar to the protection afforded under POPIA, or that the transfer is otherwise permitted by law. By using the website you acknowledge that such transfers may occur.

2.10 Information Officer and changes

The person responsible for privacy matters at Pixel Pick (Information Officer) is Rifeletsi Willie Pohleli Moloi, contactable at info@pixelpickcustomer.co.za.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website or its information practices change. The current version will be published on the website. For privacy questions or requests, contact info@pixelpickcustomer.co.za.
