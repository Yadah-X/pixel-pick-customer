Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how to request cancellation of a competition entry or address a competition that Pixel Pick needs to suspend or cancel.

4.1 Requesting cancellation

If you wish to cancel an entry, contact info@pixelpickcustomer.co.za as soon as possible and provide your account email address, competition name and order or entry reference. Pixel Pick will check whether the entry has already been confirmed and whether the related skill challenge has started or been submitted.

4.2 Entries where participation has started

Once an entry has been used to begin or complete a skill challenge, it may no longer be possible to reverse the participation activity or restore the competition position. A refund will not be provided solely because a participant has started or completed a challenge and later decides not to continue, except where a refund or cancellation is required by applicable law or the competition is cancelled by Pixel Pick.

4.3 Statutory cancellation rights (including ECT Act cooling-off)

Nothing in this policy limits a consumer's right to cancel a transaction where a statutory cancellation or cooling-off right applies. In particular, under section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002, a consumer may cancel an electronic transaction within 7 days of the date of the transaction, subject to the exceptions set out in that section (including where the service has already been fully performed with the consumer's consent). Whether a particular right applies depends on the nature of the transaction, the service provided and the relevant provisions of South African law. To exercise a cooling-off right, email info@pixelpickcustomer.co.za within the applicable period with your account and order details.

4.4 Cancellation by Pixel Pick

Pixel Pick may suspend or cancel a competition where reasonably necessary, including where there is a material technical problem, suspected manipulation, a security issue or another circumstance that prevents fair administration. Affected participants will be informed through a reasonable communication method. Any refund or remedy will be determined in accordance with the Refund Policy and applicable law.
